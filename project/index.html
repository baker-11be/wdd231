Site Plan — Mgahinga Mountain Hiking

Domain: mgahingatrekking.com

1. Site Name

Mgahinga Mountain Hiking

This name directly reflects the Mgahinga National Park in Uganda, a world renowned destination for mountain gorilla trekking and volcano hiking. It is clear, memorable, and instantly communicates the site's focus on hiking and adventure in this unique region. The name also supports local tourism branding and search visibility.

Optional domain availability: mgahingatrekking.com

2. Site Purpose

The site serves as a comprehensive information hub for anyone planning a hiking or trekking adventure in Mgahinga National Park. It provides:

  • Trail guides — detailed descriptions of the main hiking routes (e.g., Mount Gahinga, Mount Muhabura, and the bamboo forest trails).
  • Gorilla trekking information — permit booking, rules, and what to expect during a gorilla encounter.
  • Safety & preparation — packing lists, weather updates, altitude advice, and health tips.
  • Accommodation & transport — nearby lodges, campsites, and transport options to the park.

The website aims to empower visitors with practical knowledge, reduce uncertainty, and enhance their overall experience in this breathtaking volcanic landscape.

4. Color Scheme

The palette is inspired by Mgahinga's lush vegetation, volcanic soils, warm African sun, and vibrant local culture. It balances earthy reliability with energetic accents.

  • #2E5D3A — Forest Green (headings, primary navigation, main borders, strong emphasis)
  • #F5E6C8 — Cream / Warm Beige (page background, soft section backgrounds)
  • #5D4037 — Dark Brown (body text, footer areas, wireframe strokes)
  • #D4A373 — Warm Golden Sand + #E07A5F Terracotta (buttons, hover states, call-to-action highlights, card accents)

These colors are applied throughout this document — see the buttons, headings, cards, and interactive elements.

5. Typography

Two fonts are selected to create a clear hierarchy and reflect the rugged yet welcoming character of the destination.

  • Merriweather (serif, bold 700) — used for all headings (h1 h6)
  • Roboto (sans serif, regular 400 & bold 700) — used for body text, paragraphs, lists, and navigation
Heading (Merriweather)
Body text set in Roboto with a bold weight for emphasis. This combination ensures readability and a polished look on all devices.

Black and white sketches for mobile (small viewport) and desktop/tablet (wide viewport). They focus on layout structure and content priority.
