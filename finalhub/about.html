About CattleHub

Preserving Uganda's cattle heritage through sustainable agritourism.

Our Mission

CattleHub is a digital gateway to Uganda's cattle corridor. We partner with local farmers, ranches, and cultural communities to offer authentic farm visits, breed education, and immersive rural experiences.

Why Cattle?

Cattle are central to Uganda's economy and culture. From the iconic Ankole longhorns to high-yield dairy breeds, we celebrate the diversity and resilience of Ugandan livestock farming.

What We Offer

  • Curated directory of 15+ farms and ranches
  • Detailed breed information and heritage stories
  • Direct booking links and contact points
  • Educational resources for students and tourists
